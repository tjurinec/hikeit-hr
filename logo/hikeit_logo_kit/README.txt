HIKEIT LOGO KIT
===============

Included
- hikeit-horizontal.svg: primary horizontal lockup (symbol left, wordmark right)
- hikeit-stacked.svg: centered/stacked option
- hikeit-symbol.svg: standalone mountain + summit shelter symbol
- hikeit-horizontal-mono.svg: single-colour version for one-colour print/embroidery
- hikeit-horizontal-dark-bg.svg: reversed version on a dark background

Brand direction
- Charcoal: #171819
- Croatian red: #D62828
- Warm white: #F7F4EE
- Wordmark: Georgia / Times-style serif fallback

Practical notes
- SVG files are scalable vector artwork and can be opened in Illustrator, Inkscape, Figma, Affinity Designer, or a browser.
- Before commercial printing or embroidery, a designer/printer should check small-size legibility and convert text to outlines if the production workflow requires it.
- The artwork is a clean vector interpretation of the selected concept, not a trace of the generated mockup. The summit shelter and ridge are deliberately simplified so they reproduce better at small sizes.
- The SVG uses editable text for “hikeIT”; install/use a suitable serif font or convert the text to outlines before sending to a print shop.
